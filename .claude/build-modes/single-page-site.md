# Build Mode — Single-Page Site / Homepage

Loaded by the Build Mode Router in `CLAUDE.md` when the build type is a
single-page site or a standalone homepage. Applies on top of the universal
standards in `CLAUDE.md` and the chosen stack doc.

This mode prioritizes the company's overall voice and value rather than a single
campaign offer.

---

## General Notes

- Prioritize general company voice and value across the page.
- Include motion design / interaction design to make the page more engaging.

---

## Sections / Components (no required order)

- **Basic top nav** — left-aligned logo, right-aligned CTA button linked to the
  on-page CTA section.
- **Hero section** — highlights the main thing the company helps people with, in
  a clear and understandable way.
- **Who We Are section.**
- **Product / services section.**
- **Benefits section.**
- **Our Process section** (optional) — ask for details if not in the brief.
- **Why Us section** — highlights differentiators about the business.
- **Our Team section** (optional) — ask for details if not in the brief.
- **Testimonials / reviews section** (optional) — ask for details if not in the brief.
- **CTA section.**

Optional sections appear only when the brief supplies the content. Do not invent
team members, testimonials, or process steps.

---

## Mode Notes

- The single H1 is the hero headline; each section leads with an `<h2>`.
- The top-nav CTA and the CTA section share one consistent call to action.
- "Why Us" is the differentiation moment — ground it in real, supplied facts,
  not generic superlatives (see the universal Content & Copy Rules).
- Apply the behavioral-science copywriting skill to the hero, "Why Us," and CTA.
