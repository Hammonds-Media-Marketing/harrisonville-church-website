# YMYL Topic Guide & Sourcing Standards

## What is YMYL?

"Your Money or Your Life" — topics where low-quality, inaccurate, or misleading content could significantly impact a person's:
- Physical health or safety
- Financial stability
- Legal rights or obligations
- Mental health or well-being
- Major life decisions

Google applies the highest E-E-A-T standards to YMYL content because the cost of misinformation is high.

---

## YMYL Topic Categories

### High-Stakes YMYL (Maximum rigor required)
- Medical diagnoses, treatments, medications, symptoms, mental health
- Financial advice: investments, taxes, retirement, debt, insurance
- Legal guidance: contracts, rights, immigration, criminal matters
- Safety: emergency procedures, dangerous activities, child safety
- News and current events with significant societal impact
- Civic information: voting, government programs, public benefits

### Moderate YMYL (Elevated rigor required)
- Nutrition and diet (especially weight loss, supplements)
- Fitness and exercise (injury risk topics)
- Parenting and child development
- Major purchases (home buying, vehicles, high-cost products)
- Travel safety (visas, regulations, health advisories)
- Career and education decisions

### Low/No YMYL
- Hobby content (crafts, gaming, entertainment)
- Recipes and cooking (standard)
- Opinion and commentary
- Product reviews for low-stakes consumer goods
- Entertainment news and reviews

## YMYL Sourcing Standards

For High-Stakes YMYL, every factual claim must be traceable to one of the following:

**Health & Medical**
- NIH / PubMed / peer-reviewed journals
- Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, Johns Hopkins
- CDC, WHO
- Licensed medical professionals (quoted or co-authored)

**Financial**
- SEC, FINRA, IRS official documentation
- Federal Reserve publications
- CFA or CFP-credentialed sources
- Peer-reviewed financial research

**Legal**
- Official government statutes and regulations
- Bar-recognized legal resources
- Attorneys quoted or reviewed content

**Safety**
- OSHA, CPSC, NHTSA, EPA official guidance
- Government emergency management agencies

---

## YMYL Authorship Requirements

For High-Stakes YMYL content:
- Author must have verifiable credentials in the relevant field, OR
- Content must be reviewed and approved by a credentialed expert (reviewer credit required)
- Author bio must link to verifiable professional background
- Medical/legal/financial content should include a disclaimer noting it does not substitute for professional advice
